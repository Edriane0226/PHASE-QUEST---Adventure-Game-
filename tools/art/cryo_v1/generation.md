# Cryo v1 — preserved original artwork and audio

14 November 2026. Built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, two transparent outputs; no CLI/API fallback. `source-atlas.png` contains sixteen original effect frames; `source-sample.png` contains four registered water-to-ice dish states. Both source images remain unchanged. Style reference: `tools/art/phase_player_v1/current-reference.png`. No existing explorer, prop, Thermal artwork or sound was replaced or deleted.

`build.gd` packs effect cells into 512×512 RGBA, with start origins recorded in native `sprite_frames.tres` metadata. It packs four sample states into 256×256 RGBA plus `sample_frames.tres`. Runtime output is in `assets/effects/cryo/v1/`; `manifest.json` is audit metadata only. The sample dish is exclusively used by the unregistered controlled preview; it adds no campaign puzzle or unlock.

The builder also synthesizes three original 22050Hz mono PCM clips in `assets/audio/Device/cryo_{start,loop,release}.wav`. A deterministic two-second periodic combination of filtered higher-frequency components (180Hz + 13.5Hz steps, with 330/495Hz tones) gives a quieter, brighter air/crystal texture than Thermal. All frequencies complete integer cycles in two seconds; a circular sample rotation chooses a smooth seam. Imports explicitly retain PCM; no external recordings/samples or AI audio used. Runtime sets exact 0–44100 loop sample bounds, ramps gain over 40ms and uses one voice with silent attack/release edges.

Rebuild from the workspace with disposable APPDATA and LOCALAPPDATA:

```powershell
& $engine --headless --path . --script res://tools/art/cryo_v1/build.gd --quit-after 900
& $engine --headless --editor --path . --import --quit
```

The frame atlas is rendered as overlapping narrow textured filaments, not a generic line primitive. Target frost uses four authored growth stages and stays separate from the dish's four material states. Cooling motes travel away from the sample along the connection; no water or ice is created on an empty or unsuitable sample. Reduced effects removes motes while retaining beam, target frost and material-state visibility. The shared presenter owns only clocks, rendering and sound, never science outcomes.
